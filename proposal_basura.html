The 30-second pitch

Current cobots react only after safety margins are breached. We propose an embodied neurosymbolic framework fusing egocentric-exocentric vision with causal chain logic. By forecasting human intentions and hazards 2 to 5 seconds ahead, our system delivers explainable, deterministic hazard pre-emption and compliance control tailored for eldercare rehabilitation and precision manufacturing in Singapore.

What we still need to know

  • NRF CRP mandates forming a multi-disciplinary consortium with at least two Team PIs spanning robotics control and clinical/industrial domains.
  • Must avoid competing head-on against web-scale compute labs in training generic 70B+ video-language foundation models.
  • Real-time causal graph deduction on edge robotics hardware may encounter latency bottlenecks under dynamic multi-agent conditions.

Validate eligibility, novelty, and all external claims before using this in a real submission.
